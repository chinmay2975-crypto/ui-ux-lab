✦ Good questions

Before you enter

Is Orbit religious or spiritual-teacher led?

No. Sessions are secular and guided by an AI planner, not a single teacher. You choose the themes that suit you.

How long are the sessions?

From a 2-minute Quick Reset to longer evening wind-downs. Filter by length on the Meditate tab.

Do I have to make an account first?

You register once and stay signed in. Returning users get a clear "I already have an account" link on the welcome screen.

Does Orbit track streaks and points?

Orbit keeps a quiet record of your practice, with no points, badges or leaderboards pushing you to perform.

Your orbit starts with one quiet minute.

No points to chase, no streak to mourn. Just a space that meets you where today actually is.
